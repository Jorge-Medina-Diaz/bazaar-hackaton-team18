What the money bought:
- Epics from Los Picaros: 11 in the public feed after the grant against 1 before it (the pre-grant feed is incomplete: 24 of 84 settlements),
  each 137-155 P, which is more than most teams held. That was the organisers' stated aim: "Don Ernesto's vault and Los Picaros' epics are within reach".
- Epic and rare resales between teams at 195-216 P (RET-11, MAL-11, SAL-11). A trade between teams scores for both sides at their private values
  (up to +50 each): the grant financed both legs.
- Dealer-to-dealer loops (SAL-09 bought from Los Picaros, sold to Pilar): ladder slots at levels 4 and 3, the heaviest after the banco.
- t12 and t04 spent about the whole grant (net 405 and 380 P out) in the two hours that were left of round 2.
